2026 Corporate Sponsorship

Partner with SALSAL to prevent amputations.

The Save a Leg, Save a Life Foundation hosts quarterly limb-salvage roundtables and at least two community screening events a year, working to lower the rate of preventable amputations from diabetes and peripheral arterial disease. Our work runs on strong industry partnerships. Below are the ways your company can share the mission in 2026.

Every corporate sponsor, at every level, is eligible to host an exhibit table at SALSAL community screening events for a 12-month sponsorship period.
