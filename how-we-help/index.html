About · How We Help

Four ways we get between a wound and an amputation.

Prevention is not one thing. It is a chain of small, timely actions, screening, teaching, referring, advocating, that together keep a person whole.

01 · Community Screenings

Find the risk before the wound

We bring free limb screenings into community halls, senior centers, and clinics, the neighborhoods where a caught-in-time problem means a limb saved. A few minutes with a clinician can change the course of a life.

Find a screening ›
02 · Provider Education

Train the people who look first

Primary care, nursing, and frontline teams are often the first, and last, chance to catch peripheral arterial disease early. We deliver expert-led, evidence-based training so the right eyes know what to look for.

Provider education ›
03 · Patient Advocacy

Give patients their second opinion

Every person has the right to a full vascular evaluation and a second opinion before an amputation. We help patients ask the right questions and stand with them when the answer matters most.

Our advocacy work ›
04 · Free Learning

Keep everyone learning, at no cost

Our on-demand webinars and free resources put limb-preservation knowledge in reach of patients, caregivers, and clinicians alike, no login, no fee, no barrier.
