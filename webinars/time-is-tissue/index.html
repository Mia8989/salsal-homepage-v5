On-Demand Webinar · March 2026

Time Is Tissue: Disrupting Biofilm to Prevent Amputation

Matthew F. Myntti, PhD, and Dr. Matthew Regulski explore the role of biofilm in stalled wound healing and how disrupting it early can protect tissue and help prevent amputation. A science-forward look at anti-biofilm strategy in limb preservation.

Watch the recordingAll webinars ›
In this session

What you will take away

01

See why wounds stall

Understand how biofilm forms and keeps chronic wounds from progressing to healing.

02

Compare anti-biofilm strategies

Review evidence-based approaches to disrupting biofilm in the wound bed.

03

Act while there is tissue to save

Learn why early, decisive intervention is central to preventing amputation.

Your speakers

Matthew F. Myntti, PhD

Manatee Biotech

Co-Founder and Chief Science Officer at Manatee Biotech and inventor of multiple biofilm disinfection technologies. He has developed five commercially produced anti-biofilm products and was previously founder and CTO at Next Science.

Dr. Matthew Regulski, DPM

Wound Care Institute of Ocean County

Summa cum laude graduate of Temple University School of Medicine and Medical Director of the Wound Care Institute of Ocean County. He is principal investigator for multiple wound-healing trials and author of numerous peer-reviewed limb-salvage articles.
