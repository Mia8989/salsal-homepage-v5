Learning Center · Provider Education

The right eyes, in the right place, change the outcome.

Primary care, nursing, and frontline teams are often the first, and last, chance to catch peripheral arterial disease early. We help them know what to look for.

Provider education

What we teach, and how to bring it in

Recognize PAD early

Evidence-based assessment of peripheral arterial disease, so risk is caught while the outcome is still open.

Refer at the right moment

When and how to escalate, so patients reach vascular and wound specialists before a limb is lost.

A call to clinicians to put limb-preservation screening into everyday practice, one patient, one check, at a time.
