Impact · The White Sock Campaign

One white sock. One conversation that could save a limb.

Every September, PAD Awareness Month, we wear a single white sock on an exposed leg. It is small, it is strange, and that is exactly why people ask about it.

See the campaign in action

Why a white sock

A small act with a loud message

01

It points to the lower extremity

A single white sock draws the eye down, to the limb, and to the people whose lives are changed by amputation.

02

It names a real gap

It calls attention to patients who cannot afford basics like diabetic socks, the small things that protect a limb.

03

It starts the conversation

People notice. They ask. And that question is an opening to talk about peripheral arterial disease before it costs someone a leg.

How to join

Wear it. Share it. Tag us.

Put a single white sock on an exposed leg, take a photo, and post it, then tag @thesalsalorg so we can share it.
