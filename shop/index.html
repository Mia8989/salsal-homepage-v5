Good to know

Questions and answers

How does buying help SALSAL?
Every purchase supports our free community screenings, provider education, and patient resources. It is a simple way to give while getting something you will actually use.
Is my purchase tax-deductible?
No. Because you receive a product in return, a shop purchase is not a tax-deductible donation. If you would like a tax-deductible gift, please visit our Donate page.
Who makes and ships the products?
Our items are made to order through our print-on-demand partner and shipped directly to you. Because each piece is produced when you order, please allow extra time for production before it ships.
Can I return or exchange an item?
Made-to-order items are generally replaced only if they arrive damaged or defective. If anything is wrong with your order, contact us and we will help make it right.
Where can I see prices and sizing?
Prices, sizing, and shipping options are all shown at checkout in our shop. Browse the shop ›
