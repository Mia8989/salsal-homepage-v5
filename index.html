The time-out

Most amputations are not inevitable.
They are a decision.

We exist to make sure it is the right one, made with early testing, expert eyes, and every evidence-based option on the table first.

You do not have to wait for a diagnosis to act. Each of these is free, and each one is a step away from a preventable amputation.
